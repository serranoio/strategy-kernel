# Guiding Policy

Use guiding policy to define the simplest viable approach for addressing the diagnosis.

## Write The Policy

Include:

- The strategic direction.
- The constraints that must shape decisions.
- The tradeoffs that should guide design choices.
- The decision rule for choosing between plausible options.

Avoid:

- Listing implementation tasks.
- Choosing implementation details before the diagnosis supports them.
- Expanding scope beyond the stated goal without evidence.

## Quality Bar

The guiding policy should make the coherent actions feel inevitable. If multiple unrelated action plans could follow from the policy, tighten the policy until it gives clearer direction.
