# Diagnosis

Use diagnosis to establish the factual basis for the strategy kernel before proposing policy or actions.

## Sections

Use only applicable sections and omit empty sections.

### Goal

State the user's objective. Include the request, target outcome, and any explicit deadline. Avoid judging or rewriting the goal.

### Root Cause

Explain why the goal matters. Include the underlying issue, pain, risk, gap, or opportunity. Avoid jumping to implementation.

### Context

List known facts that shape the situation. Include current state, constraints, architecture constraints, dependencies, stakeholders, ownership, systems, data, timelines, and existing behavior. Avoid irrelevant background or speculation.

### Success Criteria

Define observable conditions for success. Include acceptance criteria, required outcomes, measurable behavior, and must-satisfy constraints. Avoid vague or unnecessary implementation details.

### Out of Scope

State what is explicitly excluded. Include non-goals, adjacent problems, excluded systems, features, workflows, users, or refactors. Avoid excluding anything required for success.

### Unknowns

List material facts not yet known. Include open questions, missing data, unconfirmed dependencies, and pending decisions.

### Assumptions

List unverified beliefs currently being treated as true. Include inferred facts, expected behavior, likely constraints, or working premises that affect the diagnosis.

### Challenges

List known or likely difficulties. Include risks, tradeoffs, blockers, failure modes, complications, and anything hard to verify or execute.
